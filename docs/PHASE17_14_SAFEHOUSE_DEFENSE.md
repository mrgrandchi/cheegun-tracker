# PHASE 17.14 — SAFEHOUSE DEFENSE & INFECTED THREAT

## Persistent pressure
Expeditions increase the infected threat against the Safehouse.

## Defense readiness
Readiness is calculated from Guards, Defensive Wall levels and Armory levels.

## Attack outcomes
Defense events compare Safehouse readiness against generated enemy force.
- Success lowers threat
- Failure increases structural damage and may injure a survivor

## Recovery
Safehouse damage can be repaired with credits.

## API
`CheegunDefense.raise(amount)`
`CheegunDefense.attack()`
`CheegunDefense.repair()`
`CheegunDefense.status()`
