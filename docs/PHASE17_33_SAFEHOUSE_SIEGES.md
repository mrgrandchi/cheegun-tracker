# PHASE 17.33 — SAFEHOUSE ATTACKS, SIEGES & DEFENSIVE OPERATIONS

## Threat
Safehouse threat accumulates and can escalate from warnings into infected attacks.

## Siege waves
- Scouting Infected
- Roaming Pack
- Horde Pressure
- Full Siege

## Defense
Readiness combines building defense, guard-duty assignments, legacy defense readiness, watchtower power state and assigned survivor defenders.

## Consequences
Failed defenses can cause persistent Safehouse damage, credit repair costs and survivors being marked missing.

## Emergency command
Players can assign up to six defenders, resolve an active engagement and repair accumulated structural damage.

## API
`CheegunSafehouseSieges.raise(amount,source)`
`CheegunSafehouseSieges.trigger()`
`CheegunSafehouseSieges.assignDefenders(ids)`
`CheegunSafehouseSieges.resolve()`
`CheegunSafehouseSieges.repair()`
