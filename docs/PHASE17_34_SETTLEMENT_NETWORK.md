# PHASE 17.34 — SAFEHOUSE EXPANSION, MULTIPLE SETTLEMENTS & TERRITORY COLONIZATION

## Regional expansion
The player can establish persistent secondary settlements across the Thunder Bay regional strategy layer.

## Sites
- Silver Harbour Camp — food security
- Intercity Works — industrial credits
- North Relay — intelligence
- Riverside Settlement — water resources
- Fort Sentinel — regional defense

## Progression
Each site can be colonized with credits and upgraded through three tiers. Upgrades increase population, defense and worker capacity.

## Regional economy
Specialized settlements generate resources for the wider community network.

## Strategic layer
Regional defense aggregates settlement defenses, creating a bridge toward future territory threats, logistics and inter-settlement conflicts.

## API
`CheegunSettlementNetwork.build(id)`
`CheegunSettlementNetwork.upgrade(id)`
`CheegunSettlementNetwork.assign(id,survivorId)`
`CheegunSettlementNetwork.tick()`
`CheegunSettlementNetwork.summary()`
